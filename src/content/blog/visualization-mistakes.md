# Making Data Beautiful... and Accurate

We've all seen them. Pie charts with 20 slices. Bar charts that don't start at zero. Dashboards that look like a Christmas tree.

Here are the top mistakes to avoid when designing your next Tableau or PowerBI dashboard.

## 1. Truncating the Y-Axis

This is the classic "Fox News" trick. By starting the Y-axis at 50 instead of 0, a small change looks massive. **Always start bar charts at zero.** Line charts are the exception if you are zooming in on a trend, but label it clearly.

## 2. Using Pie Charts for Everything

Humans are bad at comparing angles. We are good at comparing lengths.
*   **Bad:** Pie chart with 5+ categories.
*   **Good:** Horizontal bar chart.

## 3. Too Much Color

Color should be used to **highlight**, not to decorate. If everything is bold, nothing is bold.

*   Use gray for neutral data.
*   Use one vibrant color (blue, orange) to draw attention to the key insight.

## 4. Lack of Context

"Sales are $1M." Is that good? Bad?
Always provide context:
*   vs Last Year
*   vs Target
*   vs Competitors

> "Data without context is just noise."

## Conclusion

Great visualization isn't about making things "pretty". It's about reducing the cognitive load for your audience. Make the insight jump off the screen.
